Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about BLOCK tickets and your account.

Do I need an account to book?

Yes. Create a free BLOCK account or sign in so we can sync your bookings and tickets in one place.

Where are my tickets after I pay?

Open My Tickets from the menu. You’ll see QR codes and details for check-in.

What kinds of events do you sell?

We list sports, concerts, and other live experiences under one roof — browse everything after you sign in.

Who can I contact for help?

Visit Contact & Support or email the address listed there.
